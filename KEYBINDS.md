Artcava Keybinds
A quick reference for controlling Artcava from your keyboard or mouse.
Lyrics
Key	Action
L	Toggle synced lyrics on or off
[	Move lyric timing earlier by 0.25 seconds
]	Move lyric timing later by 0.25 seconds


Music Playback
Key	Action
A	Play the previous track
Space	Pause or resume playback
D	Play the next track


You can also click the playback controls in the top-right of the player:
On-screen control	Action
[<<]	Previous track
`[	
[>>]	Next track


Display
Key	Action
T	Toggle the song title and track information on or off


Settings Menu
Press S to open or close the settings menu.
Key	Action
↑ / ↓	Move through the settings
← / →	Decrease or increase the selected setting
Enter	Apply or increase the selected setting
S / Esc / Q	Close the settings menu


Settings changes are applied immediately and saved automatically.
General
Key	Action
Q	Quit Artcava


Tip: Playback shortcuts work while the main visualizer is active. Use the settings menu to customize Artcava without editing the configuration file manually.
